Rounded bulbous shoulders tapering outward toward the bottom, glossy cream enamel body, chrome rim around top, chrome band at bottom, two short cream feet. Wide horizontal bread slot visible along top, absolutely no bread. Soft natural studio illumination and subtle reflected highlights, realistic texture.
IMPORTANT UI compositing requirement: the front panel must be completely plain empty cream enamel, with NO levers, buttons, logos, text, gauge, knobs, writing or dial. We will overlay functional HTML controls in the same retro chrome and caramel-brown style. This is an asset with blank control face, not a final interface.
Composition: square 1024x1024 canvas, toaster centered horizontally occupying 84% width, highest point at 22% canvas height and base at 88% height. Front-on symmetrical perspective. Transparent background including below feet, soft narrow contact shadow only. No table, no scene, no borders, no text, no hand.
